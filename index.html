I am a fourth-year student in UCL's integrated master's programme (MEng) in Robotics & AI, and a Researcher in the UCL Humanoid Robotics Lab with Assoc. Prof. Chengxu Zhou. I am interested in two questions: how a humanoid can move expressively and in real time to signals it is still receiving, such as live music; and how robot policies can keep improving from their own experience rather than only from human demonstrations.

AHEADance · Streaming Humanoid Motion GenerationAHEADance · 人形机器人流式动作生成

Research in progress · UCL Humanoid Robotics Lab研究进行中 · UCL Humanoid Robotics Lab

A robot dancing to live music only hears the past, yet has to commit each move before the matching music arrives. AHEADance listens ahead with a music forecaster, generates motion chunk by chunk, and learns to continue from its own output so long dances do not fade into stillness. On a Unitree G1 with SONIC whole-body control, it produces 0.53 s of motion in about 90 ms. Next: text and keyframe guidance.

机器人随现场音乐跳舞时只能听到已经播放的部分，却必须在对应音乐到来之前做出动作。AHEADance 借助音乐预测“提前听”，逐段生成动作，并学会从自己生成的动作继续往下跳，避免长时间跳舞后动作逐渐停滞。接入 SONIC 全身控制后，在 Unitree G1 上约 90 毫秒即可生成 0.53 秒动作。下一步将加入文本与关键帧引导。

VLA / Embodied AI Algorithm Intern

VLA 具身智能算法实习生

Can a robot produce its own training data and then learn from its own mistakes? I worked on this with UTars, UBTECH's dual-arm mobile robot, on a task of moving boxes between shelves of different heights in simulation.

Data without teleoperation. A motion planner moves both arms, the torso and the base together, runs in randomly varied scenes, and keeps only clean runs as demonstrations. If the box tilts or hits the shelf, it notices, plans a fix from where the robot is, and finishes the task; these recoveries become training data too.

Learning from its own attempts. Following RECAP, a separate model learns to judge whether each moment of a run is going well, and the vision-language-action (VLA) policy is retrained to favour the good moments, with the planner taking over when it fails. After two rounds, runs ending with the box placed exactly right rose from 35% to 79%.

Moving smoothly while thinking. The policy needs time to compute its next actions, which can make the robot pause or jerk. With training-time real-time chunking, it learns to continue smoothly from the actions already sent to the robot.

机器人能不能自己生产训练数据，再从自己的失败中学习？我在优必选的 UTars 双臂移动机器人上研究这个问题，任务是在仿真中把箱子在不同高度的货架之间搬运。

不靠遥操作采数据。运动规划器同时协调双臂、躯干和底盘，在随机变化的场景中反复执行，只保留干净的轨迹作为示范。箱子倾斜或碰到货架时，它会发现问题、从当前姿态重新规划补救动作并完成任务，这些恢复过程也会成为训练数据。

从自己的尝试中学习。借鉴 RECAP，先训练一个模型判断执行过程中每一刻的进展好坏，再让 VLA（视觉-语言-动作）策略重新训练、偏向表现好的动作；策略失败时由规划器接管。两轮后，箱子严格放到位的比例从 35% 提升到 79%。

边思考边流畅动作。策略计算下一段动作需要时间，容易让机器人停顿或抖动。训练时 RTC（实时分段）让策略学会从已经发给机器人的动作自然接续。

Jun — Oct 2025 · Tsinghua IIIS × Zhongguancun Academy2025 年 6 月 — 10 月 · 清华 IIIS × 中关村学院

How can a robot learn tabletop manipulation when real demonstrations are scarce? I first trained Diffusion Policy on real-robot data, then built a simulated copy of the real workspace (a digital twin) to produce demonstrations at scale.

The main work combined the two for OpenVLA-family models: train on mixed real and simulated demonstrations, then keep improving in simulation with reinforcement learning, while the real demonstrations keep the policy close to what works on the real robot. With only 50 real demonstrations, early real-robot tests reached 68.75% success, versus 6.25% when training on real data alone.

真机示范很少时，机器人怎样学会桌面操作？我先用真机数据训练 Diffusion Policy，再在仿真中搭建与真实场景一致的“数字孪生”，大量生成示范数据。

主要工作是把两者结合起来训练 OpenVLA 系列模型：先用虚实混合示范训练，再在仿真中用强化学习继续提升，同时用真实示范约束策略，避免它在真机上的能力退化。只用 50 条真机示范时，初步真机测试成功率达到 68.75%，而只用真机数据训练为 6.25%。
