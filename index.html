Research in progress · UCL Humanoid Robotics Lab研究进行中 · UCL Humanoid Robotics Lab

A streaming motion-generation framework for Unitree G1, connecting multimodal conditioning with whole-body execution. Hierarchical diffusion generates root trajectories and full-body poses, while a discrete–continuous motion representation preserves movement structure and amplitude. Training with model-generated history reduces drift during long sequences; asynchronous inference and a SONIC whole-body control interface connect generation to execution. The current system uses music anticipation, with text instructions and keyframe constraints planned as additional controls.

面向 Unitree G1 构建流式动作生成框架，连接多模态条件与全身执行。通过分层扩散生成根轨迹和全身体态，以离散—连续协同表示保持动作结构与幅度；引入模型生成历史训练，缓解长序列动作漂移，并通过异步推理与 SONIC 全身控制接口衔接生成和执行。当前以音乐前瞻为条件，计划引入文本指令与关键帧约束，扩展动作的交互与控制方式。
