AHEADance · Streaming Humanoid Dance Generation· 人形机器人流式舞蹈生成

Manuscript in preparation · UCL Humanoid Robotics Lab论文撰写中 · UCL Humanoid Robotics Lab

A humanoid dancing to live music hears only the past, yet must commit each motion chunk before the music it accompanies arrives. AHEADance generates Unitree G1 motion chunk by chunk from streaming audio and executes it with the SONIC whole-body controller.

机器人跟着现场音乐跳舞时只能听到已经播放的部分，却必须在对应音乐到来之前提交每一段动作。AHEADance 根据实时音频逐段生成 Unitree G1 动作，并由 SONIC 全身控制器执行。

  • Listening ahead: conditions each chunk on heard music plus time-aligned forecasts from a frozen music model.
  • 音乐前瞻：每段动作同时参考已听到的音乐和冻结音乐模型的短时预测，并按目标时刻对齐。
  • Training on its own commitments: learns to continue from its own generated chunks, keeping long dances from shrinking into stillness.
  • 从自身输出中学习续接：训练时用模型自己生成的动作作为历史，避免长时间跳舞后动作逐渐收缩、停滞。
  • Real time: 90.6 ms to generate 0.53 s of motion; about 0.8 s end-to-end on the physical robot.
  • 实时：平均 90.6 毫秒生成 0.53 秒动作；真机端到端延迟约 0.8 秒。

Next: text and keyframe conditioning for multimodal control.下一步：加入文本指令与关键帧条件，实现多模态引导。
